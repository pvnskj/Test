How I operate

Senior product ownership, made visible.

The portfolio does not ask a hiring manager to infer the role from prose. The interface repeatedly exposes the artifacts and decisions expected of a senior technical product owner.

01

Frame the product goal

Make the business outcome, user/system constraint and non-negotiables explicit.

02

Make the trade-off

Show what was prioritized, what moved later, and why value, risk, effort or architecture changed the call.

03

Sequence the system

Expose dependencies, release increments, integration boundaries and readiness gates.

04

Prove the value

Separate measured, observed, projected and estimated evidence instead of flattening everything into “KPIs.”
